About

A bit about me and this community.

GamerXD_GZ is both my name online and the name of the community. This page is about the person behind it and why these servers exist.

Why I started this

I've spent a lot of time in Minecraft communities that either died out, got overrun with drama, or turned into something no fun to be part of. GamerXD_GZ started as an attempt to build the opposite of that — a small, friendly space for people who just want to hang out and play.

The LifeSteal SMP came out of that same idea: a server with real stakes, run fairly, for people already in the community rather than random public traffic.

What I care about

VibeFriendly, low-drama
ModerationActive & fair
CostFree to join, always
FocusMinecraft & gaming

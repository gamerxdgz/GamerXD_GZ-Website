About

A small, independent Minecraft project.

GamerXD_GZ isn't a company — it's one person building the tools they wanted to exist, and a server for the people who use them.

Why this exists

Most Minecraft launchers, anti-cheat plugins, and browser clients out there are either abandoned, closed-source, or come with cracked-auth baggage nobody actually wants. GamerXD_GZ started as an attempt to build the legitimate, lightweight versions of those tools instead.

The LifeSteal SMP came second — a place to actually use what's being built, with a community that gives feedback in real time.

Ground rules

AuthenticationLegitimate Microsoft only
Game filesOfficial downloads only
Assets & codeOriginal or properly licensed
MonetizationNone — free & ad-free
TelemetryNone beyond the essentials

Builds, progress updates, and the server itself all live on Discord.

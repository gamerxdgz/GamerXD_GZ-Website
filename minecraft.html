What's on the server

Built for players who like systems, not just survival.

Bounties

Put a bounty on another player's head — whoever gets the kill collects the reward straight from the server economy.

Player economy

Earn money by trading with other players or selling items through the in-game shop, then spend it however you like.

Teams

Form a team with an invite system, a shared team home, no-PvP by default, and a private channel to coordinate in.
